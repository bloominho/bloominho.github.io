About

Hello! I am an undergraduate stduent studying Electrical and Electronics Engineering at Yonsei Unviersity in Seoul, Korea.

My research interests lie in heterogeneous computing and software-hardware co-optimization. Specifically, I am interested in:

  • CPU-GPU Integration: I am interested in exploring different levels of integration between CPUs and GPUs to address fundamental inefficiencies in current architectures, where CPUs and GPUs operate as seperate devices. To resolve these problems, I am both interested in the hardware architectures and software optimization / scheduling policies of GPUs.
  • GPU Compiler Optimization: Programmers face serious complexities when optimizing their code for GPUs. I am particularly interested in developing compiler techniques that make GPU programming more accessible and enable better optimization across various GPU architectures.
Through my research, I aim to build a more powerful computing environment in response to emerging workloads.

Projects

Limitation of Optimization Targets on Loop OptimizersMarch - June 2024

Undergraduate Thesis StudentCorelab @ Yonsei University

LLVM's polyhedral optimizer, Polly, had difficulty optimizing matrix multiplication codes with dynamically bounded loops. This was because when Polly optimized its outer structures first, the transformation of (inner) nested for loops hindered effective optimization of the inner loops. To address this issue, I redesigned Polly to optimize smaller for loops first before proceeding to larger ones. As a result, a 3.5× speed improvement was achieved.

Development of near-data computing solution targeting MoE LLMs June - Aug. 2024

Undergraduate Research AssistantCAPP Lab @ Seoul National University

MoE models cannot be fully stored in GPU memory due to their large number of parameters, which leads to memory bottlenecks. To address this challenge, we developed MoNDE, a near-memory processing device. In this project, I was responsible for implementing MoNDE on an FPGA to demonstrate its performance. I also designed a protocol enabling concurrent processing and memory transfer between the FPGA device and the host, and successfully integrated it into our system. This new protocol resulted in a 1.9× speed improvement.
